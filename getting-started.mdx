Welcome! This guide will help you get started and make the most of every feature available to you.

## Getting Started

When you first log in, you'll create a company (or organization) to get started. Think of a company as your workspace where all your AI employees, content, contacts, and campaigns live.

### Your First Steps

1. **Create Your First Company** - [Learn how](/account/companies)
2. **Set Up Your AI Employees** - [Learn how](/overview/employees)
3. **Add Knowledge to Train Your AI** - [Learn how](/overview/knowledge-base)
4. **Start Chatting with Your AI** - [Learn how](/overview/chat)

## What Can You Do?

### Talk to AI Employees
Create AI employees with different personalities and expertise, then chat with them to get help with tasks, answer questions, or brainstorm ideas.
- [Chat with your AI](/overview/chat)
- [Create AI Employees](/overview/employees)
- [Give them work that runs on its own](/overview/scheduled)

### Build Your Knowledge Base
Upload documents, websites, and files to train your AI employees. The more relevant knowledge you add, the more accurate your AI becomes.
- [Knowledge Base Guide](/overview/knowledge-base)

### Find & Enrich Contacts
Build lists of contacts, enrich them with verified emails, phone numbers, and firmographic data, and run automated per-row actions.
- [Build Smart Lists](/find-enrich/smart-lists)
- [Look up and import leads](/find-enrich/lead-data)
- [See unified profiles](/find-enrich/people)

### Run Outreach Campaigns
Send personalized emails, LinkedIn touches, SMS, and more through multi-step sequences that nurture your leads and book meetings.
- [Create Sequences](/engage/sequences)
- [Manage Email Inboxes](/engage/inbox)
- [Send a one-off broadcast](/engage/marketing-campaigns)

### Create Content
Plan a strategy, research what people search for, then generate blog posts, social content, images, audio, and video — and publish on a schedule.
- [Content Engine](/engage/content-engine)
- [Find topics worth writing about](/engage/content-engine/seo-opportunities)
- [Generate Images](/engage/content-engine/images)
- [Design social graphics](/engage/content-engine/canvas)

### Answer Customers Automatically
Deploy agents that answer on your website, over the phone, and in messaging apps — capturing leads around the clock.
- [Create Agents](/inbound/agents)
- [Connect WhatsApp](/inbound/agents/whatsapp)

### Build Sites and Apps
Describe what you want and get a working site, landing page, or app — published to your own domain.
- [Build a Website](/inbound/websites)
- [Create Landing Pages](/inbound/landing-pages)
- [Build an App](/inbound/app-builder)

### Listen for Mentions
Track your brand and keywords across social platforms and AI assistants, and reply to what matters.
- [Keyword Monitoring](/engage/keyword-monitoring)

## Quick Tips

1. **Start small** — create one AI employee and test chatting before building complex campaigns
2. **Quality over quantity** — a few well-written knowledge base documents beat many low-quality ones
3. **Test your sequences** — always send yourself a test before launching a campaign
4. **Save your work** — most changes save automatically, but click Save when you see the button
5. **Use search** — most pages have search to help you find what you need quickly
