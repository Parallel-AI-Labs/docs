Welcome! This guide will help you get started and make the most of every feature available to you.

## Getting Started

When you first log in, you'll create a company (or organization) to get started. Think of a company as your workspace where all your AI employees, content, contacts, and campaigns live.

### Your First Steps

1. **Create Your First Company** - [Learn how →](/global-features/companies)
2. **Set Up Your AI Employees** - [Learn how →](/core-features/employees)
3. **Add Knowledge to Train Your AI** - [Learn how →](/core-features/knowledge-base)
4. **Start Chatting with Your AI** - [Learn how →](/core-features/chat)

## What Can You Do?

### Talk to AI Employees
Create AI employees with different personalities and expertise, then chat with them to get help with tasks, answer questions, or brainstorm ideas.
- [Chat with your AI](/core-features/chat)
- [Create AI Employees](/core-features/employees)

### Build Your Knowledge Base
Upload documents, websites, and files to train your AI employees. The more relevant knowledge you add, the more accurate your AI becomes.
- [Knowledge Base Guide](/core-features/knowledge-base)

### Find & Enrich Contacts
Build smart lists of contacts, enrich them with verified emails, phone numbers, and firmographic data, and run automated per-row actions.
- [Build Smart Lists](/workflow-features/smart-lists)

### Run Outreach Campaigns
Send personalized emails, LinkedIn touches, SMS, and more through multi-step sequences that nurture your leads and book meetings.
- [Create Sequences](/workflow-features/sequences)
- [Manage Email Inboxes](/workflow-features/inbox)

### Create Content
Generate blog posts, social content, images, audio, and videos with AI — and publish them on a schedule.
- [Content Engine](/content-tools/content-engine)
- [Generate Images](/content-tools/images)
- [Create Audio](/content-tools/audio)
- [Generate Videos](/content-tools/video)

### Deploy Website Agents
Create AI agents you can embed on your website to answer questions and capture leads 24/7.
- [Create Agents](/core-features/agents)

## Navigation Guide

### The Sidebar Menu

After selecting a company, the sidebar groups features into collapsible sections. Here's what you'll find:

**At the top**

| Item | What It's For |
|------|---------------|
| Employees | Create and manage your AI employees |
| Chat | Talk to your AI employees |
| Knowledge Base | Upload training documents |
| Dashboard | See your analytics and metrics |

**Find & Enrich**

| Item | What It's For |
|------|---------------|
| [Smart Lists](/workflow-features/smart-lists) | Manage contact lists and run per-row actions |
| [Enrichment](/workflow-features/enrichment) | Look up and enrich contact data |

**Engage**

| Item | What It's For |
|------|---------------|
| [Sequences](/workflow-features/sequences) | Run multi-step outreach campaigns |
| [Inbox](/workflow-features/inbox) | Manage connected email accounts and replies |
| [Content Engine](/content-tools/content-engine) | Plan and generate marketing content |
| [Marketing Campaigns](/workflow-features/marketing-campaigns) | Send one-time broadcast emails to a list |
| [Keyword Monitoring](/workflow-features/keyword-monitoring) | Track keywords and brand mentions |

**Inbound**

| Item | What It's For |
|------|---------------|
| [Agents](/core-features/agents) | Build website chatbots powered by your AI employees |
| [Landing Pages](/inbound-apps/landing-pages) | Create and publish landing pages |
| [App Builder](/inbound-apps/app-builder) | Build and publish custom apps |

**Settings**

| Item | What It's For |
|------|---------------|
| Integrations | Connect external tools and services |
| Collaborators | Invite team members and manage roles |
| Profile | Your account, subscription, and security |
| Support | Get help |

> The Content Engine opens a sub-menu with **Images**, **Audio**, **Video**, and **Voices** for standalone media creation. The exact items you see depend on your plan and which features are enabled for your account.

## Quick Tips

1. **Start small** — create one AI employee and test chatting before building complex campaigns
2. **Quality over quantity** — a few well-written knowledge base documents beat many low-quality ones
3. **Test your sequences** — always send yourself a test before launching a campaign
4. **Save your work** — most changes save automatically, but click Save when you see the button
5. **Use search** — most pages have search to help you find what you need quickly
