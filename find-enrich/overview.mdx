Everything in this section answers one question — *who are we talking to?* Start from a Smart List you build by hand, or pull contacts in from external data sources, then enrich them until you have enough to run outreach.

## In This Section

- **[People](/find-enrich/people)** — Unified profiles that merge the same person across Smart Lists, sequences, campaigns, conversations, and your inbox.
- **[Smart Lists](/find-enrich/smart-lists)** — Spreadsheet-style tables of contacts and data, with per-row AI actions.
- **[Lead Data](/find-enrich/lead-data)** — Look up and import contacts from external sources: Enrichment, People Search, Sales Navigator, and the credits that pay for them.

## Where Contacts Come From

| Route | Best for |
|-------|----------|
| Type or paste into a [Smart List](/find-enrich/smart-lists) | Contacts you already have |
| [Enrichment](/find-enrich/lead-data/enrichment) | Filling in details for one known person or company |
| [People Search](/find-enrich/lead-data/people-search) | Building an audience from scratch using filters |
| [Sales Navigator](/find-enrich/lead-data/sales-navigator) | Turning a LinkedIn search into a list |
| A CRM [integration](/settings/integrations) | Syncing contacts you keep elsewhere |

All of them land in the same place: a Smart List you can enrich, score, and feed into a [sequence](/engage/sequences).
