**Company Brain** is the shared context that helps your AI employees understand your business. During the beta, its first layer connects people, companies, relationships, and activity from across your Parallel AI workspace.

The same person often appears in several places — a row in a Smart List, a recipient in a sequence, someone who replied in your inbox, or a visitor who chatted with an agent. Company Brain resolves those records into one profile so you can see what your company knows about that person in one place.

> **Beta:** Company Brain is available to every member who has access to the company.

## What Company Brain Knows Today

The current beta brings together:

- **People** — unified profiles built from records across your workspace
- **Companies** — company details and the people associated with them
- **Relationships** — connections between people, companies, and source records
- **Engagement** — replies, messages, calls, clicks, scheduling activity, and other events
- **Source evidence** — the records and identifiers used to connect everything

Company Brain is designed to expand beyond identity and engagement into broader business knowledge, including documents, processes, products, and other context your AI employees can use.

## How the People Layer Is Built

Profiles are assembled by a background job that runs nightly. It looks for **identifiers** shared between records and merges the records that match.

Identifiers come in two strengths:

| Identifier | Strength | Why |
|------------|----------|-----|
| **Email** | Strong | Unique to one person |
| **Phone** | Strong | Unique to one person |
| **LinkedIn** | Strong | Unique to one person |
| **Name + Company** | Weak | Names collide — two people called Chris Taylor at the same firm are indistinguishable |

Strong identifiers merge records with confidence. A **Name + Company** match is treated as low confidence, so the profile is added to **Identity review** for confirmation.

On a profile's **Data sources** tab, you can review the matching evidence, confirm an identity, or disable an incorrect or shared identifier so it does not create future matches. Correct the underlying source record when profile data itself is wrong; the next sync picks up the change.

## Browsing Company Brain

1. Select your company from the sidebar
2. Click **Company Brain** under **Find & Enrich**

The People table shows **Name**, **Email**, **Company**, **Sources**, and **Latest engagement**. Use the tabs to switch between **All people**, **Engaged**, **No engagement yet**, and **Identity review**.

- **Search** matches name, email, phone, or company
- **Source** filters to people who appear in a given place: Smart List, Sequence, Campaign, Conversation, or Inbox
- **Sort by** orders people by latest engagement or name
- **Sources** shows how many linked records came from each part of the workspace
- Select a person to open the full profile, or use the preview action to inspect them without leaving the table

## Reading a Person Profile

The full profile combines:

- **About this person** — contact details, role, location, and other known properties
- **Engagement** — the most important human interactions, ordered over time
- **All activity** — the broader timeline of recorded events
- **Data sources** — identifiers and matching evidence used to build the profile
- **Company** — firmographic details and other people associated with the company
- **Source records** — the Smart Lists, sequences, campaigns, conversations, inbox messages, and CRM records linked to the person

### Engagement and Activity

Company Brain distinguishes meaningful human engagement from background activity. Replies, inbound messages, calls, clicks, scheduling views, and bookings can contribute to a person's engagement history. Sends, enrollments, and opens remain available in the broader activity record but do not rank someone as actively engaged.

Automated traffic, such as email scanners and image proxies, is identified so it is not mistaken for human interest.

## Settings and Sources

Select the settings icon on the Company Brain page to manage:

- **People sync** — refresh people and company data, including your selected CRM
- **Activity sync** — bring Calendar, Zoom, and other connected-app events into person timelines
- **Data sources** — choose the CRM connection that contributes shared business context

Smart Lists, sequences, campaigns, conversations, and inbox activity are always-on sources.

## What Company Brain Is Good For

- Checking whether you're already talking to someone before starting fresh
- Seeing every prior touch before a sales call
- Spotting the same person sitting in three different lists under two email addresses
- Understanding which channel a lead actually engages with
- Giving AI employees a consistent, company-wide view of the people and businesses you work with
