People Search finds professionals who fit your criteria. Search results show name, company, and location. Search does not perform contact or company enrichment.

## Building a Search

1. Select your company from the sidebar.
2. Open **People Search** under Find & Enrich.
3. Expand the filter groups and enter your criteria.
4. Click **Search** to see matching people and the total match count.
5. Browse pages of 5, 10, 25, or 50 people. Click a name to view its details or open its LinkedIn profile.

Enter at least one positive filter. Multiple values within a filter match any of those values; different filters combine. Use **Reset** to clear the search.

## Filter Groups

| Group | Examples |
|-------|----------|
| **Person** | Job titles and exclusions, job levels, job functions, education, LinkedIn connections |
| **Company** | Names, domains, LinkedIn URLs, industries, employee ranges, revenue, website traffic, company keywords |
| **Funding** | Funding types, investors, total funding, latest funding amount and year |
| **Location** | Person and company headquarters cities, country codes, continents, sales regions |

Choose the supplied dropdown values for job levels, functions, industries, and other enumerated filters. Country filters use two-letter codes such as `US` or `GB`. Leave numeric bounds blank or set them to zero to omit that bound.

Job-title searches can include LinkedIn headlines. Excluded titles help remove unwanted roles, such as assistants or interns.

## Importing and Exporting

Click **Import** to add people to a Smart List, or **Export** to get a CSV file. Choose how many people to include:

- **Selected people** — the people you've checked on the current page (people need a LinkedIn URL or email to be selected)
- **Up to** a set number, or a **custom number** — taken in order from the first result, across pages
- **All** matching people, up to 50,000

Then choose what to look up for each person:

- **Include email** (on by default) — finds their email address and profile details
- **Include mobile/direct phone number** — finds a mobile or direct phone number

Imports and exports run in the background. You can leave the page; you'll get an email when the job is ready, and an export's email includes a CSV download link that stays valid for 24 hours. If you run low on data credits partway through, the job waits and resumes once credits are available.

When importing, choose the destination Smart List. People already in that list with the same LinkedIn URL or email are updated instead of duplicated.

Import a small sample first to check that your criteria find the right people.

## Credits

Searching is **free**. To search, you need a balance of **at least 1 data credit**, or remaining capacity in your plan's data-credit window. Search never spends it, including when you load more pages.

Imports and exports cost:

- **1 data credit** per person whose email is found
- **2 data credits** per phone number found, when phone numbers are included

People with no match cost nothing. See [Credits](/find-enrich/lead-data/lead-credits).
