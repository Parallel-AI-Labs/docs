People Search finds professionals who fit your criteria. Search results show name, company, and location. Search does not perform contact or company enrichment.

## Building a Search

1. Select your company from the sidebar.
2. Open **People Search** under Find & Enrich.
3. Expand the filter groups and enter your criteria.
4. Click **Search** to see matching people and the total match count.
5. Browse pages of 5, 10, or 25 people. Click a name to view its details or open its LinkedIn profile.

Enter at least one positive filter. Multiple values within a filter match any of those values; different filters combine. Use **Reset** to clear the search.

## Filter Groups

| Group | Examples |
|-------|----------|
| **Person** | Job titles and exclusions, job levels, job functions, education, LinkedIn connections |
| **Company** | Names, domains, LinkedIn URLs, industries, employee ranges, revenue, website traffic, company keywords |
| **Funding** | Funding types, investors, total funding, latest funding amount and year |
| **Location** | Person and company headquarters cities, country codes, continents, sales regions |

Choose the supplied dropdown values for job levels, functions, industries, and other enumerated filters. Country filters use two-letter codes such as `US` or `GB`. Leave numeric bounds blank or set them to zero to omit that bound.

Job-title searches can include LinkedIn headlines. Excluded titles help remove unwanted roles, such as assistants or interns.

The separate **advanced people-search dialog** continues to offer its consumer and intent filters. Those filters are not available in the professional People Search page or public lead tools.

## Exporting and Importing

**Export** downloads selected people, or the current page when nothing is selected, as a CSV file.

To save contacts, select the people you want on the current page and click **Import**. Choose an existing Smart List or create a new one. Contacts need a LinkedIn URL or email to be imported. Missing columns can be created automatically, and existing contacts can be matched by LinkedIn URL. Import looks up available email addresses. Company details and phones are not fetched during import.

Selections apply to the current page. Import a small sample first to check that your criteria find the right people.

## Credits

People Search and export are **free**. Searching requires a balance of **at least 1 lead data credit**, including when requesting another results page. The credit is not consumed by search. Import costs **1 data credit per successfully imported person** and enriches their email. See [Credits](/find-enrich/lead-data/lead-credits).

Public lead imports run in the background, cost one data credit per imported contact, and support a requested count. They stop at 50,000 records. Public API and MCP clients can use **lead field options** to discover the accepted filter names and enum values.
