**Leads** is where you find prospects and enrich contact details before importing them into a Smart List. **People** in Company Brain holds the saved profiles, relationships, and activity across your workspace. Its sub-pages cover three ways to find people, how to do the same through the API or MCP, and what it all costs.

## In This Section

- **[Enrichment](/find-enrich/lead-data/enrichment)** — Look up one contact or company by any identifier you have and get back verified details.
- **[People Search](/find-enrich/lead-data/people-search)** — Build an audience from filters — industry, seniority, location, company size, and more.
- **[Sales Navigator](/find-enrich/lead-data/sales-navigator)** — Turn a LinkedIn Sales Navigator search into a list of leads.
- **[Leads via API & MCP](/find-enrich/lead-data/api-and-mcp)** — Run search, import, export, and enrichment from your own code or an AI assistant.
- **[Credits](/find-enrich/lead-data/lead-credits)** — Your data credit balance and what each lookup costs.

## Which One Should I Use?

| You have | Use |
|----------|-----|
| A person's email, phone, or LinkedIn URL | [Enrichment](/find-enrich/lead-data/enrichment) |
| A company name or domain, and you want people there | [Enrichment](/find-enrich/lead-data/enrichment) — it previews matching people |
| A description of your ideal customer, but no names | [People Search](/find-enrich/lead-data/people-search) |
| A Sales Navigator search you've already built | [Sales Navigator](/find-enrich/lead-data/sales-navigator) |

All three save their results into a [Smart List](/find-enrich/smart-lists), so the next step is the same no matter which you use.

## About Data Credits

Enrichment, People Search, and Sales Navigator all draw on one shared pool of **data credits**. Credits never expire. See [Credits](/find-enrich/lead-data/lead-credits) for balances, costs, and how to top up.

If your plan includes lead data, there is nothing to buy and the Credits page won't appear in your menu.
