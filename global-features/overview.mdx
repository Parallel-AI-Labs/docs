These features apply across your entire account, no matter which company you're working in.

## In This Section

- **[Companies](/global-features/companies)** — Create, switch between, reorder, and manage the companies (workspaces) on your account.
- **[Profile](/global-features/profile)** — Manage your personal details, subscription, credits, notifications, and security settings like multi-factor authentication.
- **[Support](/global-features/support)** — Submit and track support tickets, and find help when you need it.

Your login, subscription, credit balance, and security settings follow you across every company you have access to.
