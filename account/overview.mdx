Your login, subscription, credit balance, and security settings belong to you, not to any one company. Switching companies doesn't change them.

## In This Section

- **[Companies](/account/companies)** — Create, switch between, reorder, and manage your companies.
- **[Profile](/account/profile)** — Your personal details, subscription, credits, notifications, and security settings.
- **[Support](/account/support)** — Submit and track support tickets.

## Companies vs. Your Account

A **company** is a workspace: its own AI employees, lists, content, integrations, and collaborators. Your **account** is you — one login that can hold several companies.

That's why credits and subscription live on your [Profile](/account/profile) rather than inside a company, while integrations and collaborators are set per company.

How many companies you can create depends on your plan — see [Companies](/account/companies).
