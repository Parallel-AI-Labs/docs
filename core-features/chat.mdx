The Chat feature lets you have conversations with your AI employees. Ask questions, get help with tasks, brainstorm ideas, or just explore what your AI can do.

---

## Starting a Conversation

1. Select your company from the sidebar
2. Click **Chat**
3. Choose an AI employee to talk to (you'll see their profile cards)
4. Type your message in the text box at the bottom
5. Press **Enter** or click the send button

Your AI employee will respond based on their personality and the knowledge you've given them.

---

## Choosing the Right AI Employee

Each AI employee has different expertise based on how you've set them up. Choose the one that fits your question:

- **Support-focused employee:** Ask about customer questions, policies, procedures
- **Sales-focused employee:** Get help with pitches, objections, product info
- **Technical employee:** Ask about processes, how things work
- **General assistant:** Good for brainstorming and general questions

**Tip:** You can switch employees mid-conversation if you need a different perspective.

## Sending Messages

### Text Messages

Just type your question or request and press Enter. Be specific for better responses:

| Instead of... | Try... |
|---------------|--------|
| "Help me" | "Help me write an email to a customer who's upset about a late delivery" |
| "What do you know?" | "What do you know about our return policy?" |
| "Write something" | "Write a LinkedIn post about our new product launch" |

### Attaching Images

Some AI employees can analyze images:

1. Click the **attachment icon** (paperclip or image icon)
2. Select an image from your computer
3. Add a message explaining what you want to know about the image
4. Send

Example: Attach a screenshot and ask "What's wrong with this design?"

### Uploading Documents

Share documents for context:

1. Click the **upload icon**
2. Select a file (PDF, Word doc, etc.)
3. Ask your question about the document
4. Send

Example: Upload a contract and ask "Summarize the key terms in this agreement."

## Getting Better Responses

### Be Specific

The more context you provide, the better the response:

❌ "Write an email"
✅ "Write a follow-up email to a prospect who attended our webinar last week but hasn't scheduled a demo. Keep it friendly and under 100 words."

### Provide Context

Tell the AI what you're trying to accomplish:

❌ "What should I say?"
✅ "A customer is threatening to cancel because of a billing error. What should I say to keep them as a customer?"

### Ask Follow-Up Questions

The AI remembers the conversation, so build on previous responses:

1. "What are the benefits of our premium plan?"
2. "Now write that as a comparison table"
3. "Make it more concise"

## Conversation Features

### Starting Fresh

To begin a new conversation:

1. Click the **Reset** or **New Chat** button
2. Your conversation history is cleared
3. Start your new conversation

### Viewing Chat History

To see past conversations:

1. Click **Chat History** or the history icon
2. Browse your previous chats
3. Click on one to continue it

### Sharing a Conversation

To share a conversation with a colleague:

1. Click the **Share** button
2. Copy the generated link
3. Send it to whoever needs to see it

They'll be able to view the conversation (but not continue it).

## Adjusting Chat Settings

Click the **Settings** icon (gear) to customize your chat experience:

### Model Selection

Choose which AI model powers your conversation:
- **Fast models:** Quick responses, good for simple questions
- **Advanced models:** Slower but more thoughtful, good for complex tasks

### Context Settings

Control how much context the AI uses:
- More context = better understanding but uses more credits
- Less context = faster and cheaper but may miss nuance

### Connected Tools

Tools let your AI employee take action — not just answer. In the chat settings, click the **Connected Tools** card (it shows how many tools are currently enabled) to turn tools on or off for your conversation.

Built-in tools:

| Tool | What It Does |
|------|--------------|
| **Enable platform API tools** | Manage lists, sequences, agents, and other platform resources via API |
| **Research Task** | Delegate complex research tasks to a specialized research agent *(paid plans; +2 credits)* |
| **Create Documents** | Generate PDF, DOCX, or CSV files and email them to you |
| **Browser Automation** | Automate a real web browser for web scraping, form filling, and navigating websites — click the tool to choose which browser session it uses |
| **Leads & Enrichment** | Find leads by demographic, firmographic, and buyer-intent filters, and enrich people or companies with contact info, demographics, and social profiles *(availability depends on your plan; uses data credits)* |
| **Delegate Task** | Hand subtasks to your other AI employees, using their specialized skills and tools |

Alongside the built-in tools you'll see your **connected tools** — MCP servers, connected apps, and toolsets set up in [Integrations](/admin-features/integrations) — each toggled per conversation. From here you can also browse the catalog of 1000+ app integrations (Gmail, Slack, HubSpot, Salesforce, and hundreds more) or click **Manage Toolsets** to group tools into reusable sets.

When the AI uses a tool, the step appears right in the conversation (for example, **Find Leads** or **Enrich Contact**) so you can follow what it's doing.

> Tools require a tool-compatible model — if your current model doesn't support tools, pick a different one in settings. The exact set of tools you see depends on your plan and enabled features.

## Tips for Effective Conversations

1. **One topic at a time** - Don't mix unrelated questions in one message
2. **Use examples** - Show the AI what you want: "Write something like this: [example]"
3. **Give feedback** - If a response isn't right, explain what to change
4. **Be patient** - Complex questions take longer to answer
5. **Experiment** - Try different ways of asking to find what works best

## Understanding AI Responses

### When the AI Says "I Don't Know"

This usually means:
- The information isn't in the knowledge base
- The question is outside the AI's training
- You need to add more documents to help the AI learn

**Solution:** Add relevant documents to your [Knowledge Base](/core-features/knowledge-base).

### When Responses Seem Wrong

AI can make mistakes. If something seems off:
- Ask the AI to double-check its answer
- Verify important information yourself
- Report issues to improve the AI over time

### When Responses Are Too Long/Short

Guide the AI with explicit instructions:
- "Give me a brief 2-sentence answer"
- "Explain this in detail with examples"
- "Summarize in bullet points"

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Does the AI remember previous conversations?**
A: Within a single session, yes. But when you start a new conversation, it begins fresh.

**Q: Who can see my conversations?**
A: By default, only you. Shared conversations can be viewed by anyone with the link.

**Q: How do I make the AI sound more like me/my brand?**
A: Customize the AI employee's personality and instructions in the [Employees](/core-features/employees) settings.

**Q: Why is the AI giving wrong information about my company?**
A: The AI only knows what's in your [Knowledge Base](/core-features/knowledge-base). Add more documents with accurate information.

**Q: Can I use the AI for customer-facing chat?**
A: Yes! Create an [Agent](/core-features/agents) to embed on your website.
