The essential tools for working with your AI employees day to day — from chatting with them to training them and deploying them on your website.

## In This Section

- **[Dashboard](/core-features/dashboard)** — Your analytics home: activity, usage, and performance across every feature you use.
- **[Chat](/core-features/chat)** — Talk directly with your AI employees to get work done, ask questions, and brainstorm.
- **[AI Employees](/core-features/employees)** — Create and customize the AI team members that power chat, agents, and automations.
- **[Knowledge Base](/core-features/knowledge-base)** — Upload the documents, websites, and files that train your AI employees.
- **[Agents](/core-features/agents)** — Deploy website chatbots powered by your AI employees to answer visitor questions and capture leads.

> **AI Employees vs. Agents:** An **AI Employee** is the assistant you chat with and configure inside the app. An **Agent** is a public-facing chatbot you embed on your website — each agent is powered by one of your AI employees.
