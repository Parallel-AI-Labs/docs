Turn text into natural-sounding speech using AI voice technology. Create voiceovers for videos, podcasts, training materials, phone systems, and more.

## What Can You Create?

- Video narration and voiceovers
- Podcast intros and outros
- Training and educational content
- IVR phone messages
- Audiobook content
- Voice announcements
- Accessibility audio versions of content

## Creating Your First Audio

### Step 1: Open Audio Generation

1. Select your company from the sidebar
2. Click **Audio** (under the Content Engine menu)
3. Click **Generate Audio** (or the **+** button)

### Step 2: Enter Your Text

Type or paste the text you want converted to speech:

```
Welcome to our company! We're excited to have you here.
In this video, we'll walk you through the key features
that will help you get started quickly.
```

### Step 3: Choose a Voice

Browse available voices and pick one that fits your needs:
- Male or female
- Different accents (American, British, etc.)
- Various personality types (friendly, professional, energetic)

You can choose from ready-made voices or select a **custom voice you've cloned** in the [Voices](/engage/content-engine/voices) section — your cloned voices appear right alongside the ready-made ones.

**Tip:** Click the play button next to a voice to hear a sample.

### Step 4: Adjust Settings

- **Speed:** How fast the voice speaks (0.5x to 2x)
- **Model:** Different AI models for varying quality

### Step 5: Generate

1. Click **Generate**
2. Wait a few seconds
3. Play your audio to review

Happy with it? Download and use it. Need changes? Adjust and regenerate.

## Writing for Audio

Text written for reading doesn't always sound natural when spoken. Here are tips for better audio:

### Write Conversationally

**Avoid — Written style:**
"The implementation of our solution facilitates operational efficiency."

**Better — Audio style:**
"Our solution makes your work faster and easier."

### Use Punctuation for Pacing

Punctuation affects how the AI reads your text:
- **Commas:** Short pause
- **Periods:** Medium pause
- **Dashes (—):** Dramatic pause
- **Ellipses (...):** Thoughtful pause

**Example:**
```
This is important... really important.
And here's why — our customers love it.
```

### Spell Out Abbreviations

The AI might mispronounce abbreviations:
- **Avoid:** "CEO" might be read as "see-ee-oh"
- **Better:** "Chief Executive Officer" or write phonetically

### Add Phonetic Hints for Unusual Words

For names or technical terms:
```
John Scieszka (pronounced "SHESS-ka")
```

## Adjusting Speed

The speed setting affects how fast the voice speaks:

| Speed | Best For |
|-------|----------|
| **0.75x** | Detailed explanations, emphasis, tutorials |
| **1.0x** | Normal conversation, most content |
| **1.25x** | Quick announcements, summaries |
| **1.5x** | Fast-paced content, short messages |

**Tip:** Most professional voiceovers use 0.9x to 1.1x speed.

2. Use the play controls

1. Click the **three-dot menu** (⋮) on the audio file

3. Confirm

## Tips for Better Audio

### 1. Break Long Text into Sections

Instead of one long script, create separate audio files for:
- Intro
- Main content sections
- Call to action
- Outro

This makes editing easier and allows mixing voices.

### 2. Test Before Full Production

Generate a short sample first to verify:
- The voice sounds right
- Speed is appropriate
- Pronunciation is correct
- Tone matches your content

### 3. Review and Re-Generate

Listen critically:
- Does it sound natural?
- Are pauses in the right places?
- Is the emphasis correct?

Adjust text or settings and regenerate as needed.

### 4. Consider Your Audience

- **International audience:** Choose a neutral accent
- **Technical content:** Slower speed for comprehension
- **Young audience:** More energetic voices

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How long does audio generation take?**
A: Usually 5-15 seconds for short clips, up to a minute for longer content.

**Q: What format are audio files?**
A: Typically MP3. Some systems offer additional formats.

**Q: Can I use these for commercial purposes?**
A: Yes, check your subscription terms for specific commercial use rights.

**Q: Why does the voice mispronounce a word?**
A: Try spelling it phonetically or differently. AI learns from common pronunciations.

**Q: Can I make the voice sound emotional?**
A: To some extent. Use punctuation and word choice to guide emotion. For more control, consider using custom [Voices](/engage/content-engine/voices).
