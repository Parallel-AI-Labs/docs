Your brand voice is the personality, tone, and style of your writing. Setting one means you stop editing the same things out of every AI draft — the corporate buzzwords, the wrong level of formality, the phrases you'd never actually use.

## Creating a Brand Voice

1. Select your company from the sidebar
2. Go to **Content Engine** → **Brand Voice**
3. Click **New Brand Voice** — or **Generate with AI** to infer a voice from your existing material
4. Define:
   - **Personality** — professional, casual, playful, authoritative?
   - **Tone** — friendly, formal, conversational?
   - **Writing style** — simple, technical, storytelling?
   - **Words to use** — terminology and phrases you like
   - **Words to avoid** — language that doesn't fit your brand
5. Save

The "words to avoid" list does more work than people expect. It's the difference between drafts you edit and drafts you publish.

## Example

```
Personality: Friendly expert
Tone: Warm but professional
Style: Clear and direct, avoid jargon

DO use:
- "You" and "your" to speak directly to readers
- Simple explanations for complex topics
- Real examples and stories

DON'T use:
- Corporate buzzwords like "synergy" or "leverage"
- Overly casual language like "gonna" or "wanna"
- Fear-based marketing tactics
```

## Managing Brand Voices

The list shows each voice's **Name**, **Industry**, **Target Audience**, and when it was created and updated. Use a voice's menu to **View Details**, **Edit**, or **Delete**.

You can keep more than one — a formal voice for whitepapers and a looser one for social, for instance — and point different [campaigns](/engage/content-engine/content-campaigns) at different voices.

## Where It's Used

A brand voice applies anywhere the AI writes for you: generated [content](/engage/content-engine/content), [campaign](/engage/content-engine/content-campaigns) output, and drafts produced from an [opportunity](/engage/content-engine/seo-opportunities) brief.

## Tips

1. **Show, don't just label.** "Warm but professional" means less than two example sentences demonstrating it.
2. **Be specific about what to avoid.** Naming the exact words you hate works better than "avoid jargon."
3. **Derive it from real writing.** Point **Generate with AI** at material you already like, then refine.
4. **Keep it short enough to be followed.** A voice guide nobody can hold in their head is a voice guide the output won't reflect either.
