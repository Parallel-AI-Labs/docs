Create custom images using AI text-to-image technology. Perfect for social media graphics, blog illustrations, marketing materials, and more.

## What Can You Create?

- Marketing graphics and banners
- Social media images
- Blog post illustrations
- Product mockups
- Abstract backgrounds
- Conceptual artwork
- And more!

## Generating Your First Image

### Step 1: Open Image Generation

1. Select your company from the sidebar
2. Click **Images** (under the Content Engine menu)
3. Click **Generate Image** (or the **+** button)

### Step 2: Write Your Prompt

Describe the image you want. The more detail, the better:

**Basic prompt:**
```
A modern office workspace
```

**Better prompt:**
```
A bright, modern office workspace with large windows,
a wooden desk with a laptop, green plants, and soft
natural lighting. Minimalist style, warm colors.
```

### Step 3: Choose Settings

- **Model:** Different AI models create different styles
- **Size:** Square (1024x1024), landscape, or portrait
- **Style:** Vivid (bold colors) or Natural (realistic)
- **Quality:** Standard or HD

### Step 4: Generate

1. Click **Generate**
2. Wait a few seconds
3. Your image appears!

Not quite right? Adjust your prompt and generate again.

## Writing Great Prompts

### Include These Elements

| Element | Example |
|---------|---------|
| **Subject** | "A golden retriever" |
| **Action/Pose** | "running through a field" |
| **Setting** | "in a sunny meadow" |
| **Style** | "photorealistic style" |
| **Lighting** | "golden hour lighting" |
| **Mood** | "joyful and energetic" |
| **Colors** | "warm autumn colors" |

### Example Prompts

**For a business blog:**
```
Professional business team having a productive meeting
in a modern glass conference room. Diverse group of
people, natural lighting, corporate but friendly
atmosphere. Clean, professional photography style.
```

**For social media:**
```
Flat lay photography of a coffee cup, notebook,
and succulent plant on a white marble desk.
Minimalist aesthetic, soft shadows, Instagram-worthy
product photography style.
```

**For abstract backgrounds:**
```
Abstract flowing gradient background in soft purple
and blue tones. Smooth, professional, suitable for
website header. Modern and clean design.
```

## Understanding Image Settings

### Models

Different models excel at different styles:

| Model | Best For |
|-------|----------|
| **GPT Image 1 / 1.5 / 2** | General purpose, high quality, and precise edits |
| **FLUX.1 Kontext** | Controllable generation and editing |
| **Leonardo (Phoenix, Kino XL)** | Strong prompt adherence and text rendering |
| **Flux Schnell** | Fast drafts |
| **Nano Banana (Google)** | Google's Gemini image model |

The exact model list depends on your plan. Try a few to see which works best for your needs. Some models (like the GPT Image family and Leonardo) also accept **reference images** so you can guide the result or edit an existing image.

### Sizes

| Size | Dimensions | Best For |
|------|------------|----------|
| **Square** | 1024x1024 | Social media, thumbnails |
| **Landscape** | 1792x1024 | Blog headers, banners |
| **Portrait** | 1024x1792 | Stories, Pinterest |

### Style Options

- **Vivid:** Bold, dramatic colors and high contrast
- **Natural:** More realistic, subdued tones

### Quality

- **Standard:** Faster, good for drafts and testing
- **HD:** Higher detail, best for final outputs

## Using Reference Images

Some models let you use existing images as inspiration:

### How to Use References

1. Click **Add Reference Image**
2. Upload an image or select from your library
3. Describe how you want to modify or combine elements
4. Generate

**Example:**
Upload your product photo and prompt: "Create a lifestyle scene with this product on a modern kitchen counter with morning coffee and fresh fruit."

## Managing Your Images

### Viewing Your Library

All generated images are saved automatically:

1. Go to **Images**
2. Browse your gallery
3. Click on any image to view full size

### Downloading Images

1. Click on an image
2. Click **Download**
3. Image saves to your computer

### Copying to Clipboard

1. Click on an image
2. Click **Copy**
3. Paste anywhere (documents, social media, etc.)

### Deleting Images

1. Click the **three-dot menu** (⋮) on an image
2. Select **Delete**
3. Confirm

## Tips for Better Images

### 1. Be Specific

Generic prompts get generic results. Add details about:
- Colors and color scheme
- Lighting and atmosphere
- Composition and framing
- Style and artistic approach

### 2. Iterate

Your first image is rarely perfect. Try:
- Adjusting your prompt slightly
- Changing the style setting
- Using a different model
- Adding or removing details

### 3. Use Style Keywords

Add these to your prompts:
- "Professional photography"
- "Minimalist design"
- "Flat illustration"
- "Watercolor painting"
- "3D render"
- "Vintage aesthetic"

### 4. Avoid Common Issues

**Hands and text:** AI often struggles with these. If needed, edit them out or plan to avoid them.

**Faces:** For professional use, verify faces look natural. Consider abstract or distant subjects.

**Brands and logos:** AI shouldn't reproduce copyrighted content. Create original designs instead.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How long does image generation take?**
A: Usually 10-30 seconds depending on settings and demand.

**Q: Why doesn't my image match what I described?**
A: Try adding more specific details, rephrasing, or breaking complex ideas into simpler prompts.

**Q: Can I edit generated images?**
A: Currently, the platform generates images but doesn't include editing. Download and use your preferred image editor.

**Q: Are there content restrictions?**
A: Yes. The AI won't generate inappropriate, harmful, or copyrighted content.

**Q: Can I generate images of real people?**
A: No. Creating images of specific real people without consent isn't supported for privacy and ethical reasons.
