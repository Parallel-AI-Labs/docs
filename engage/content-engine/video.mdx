Create videos using AI - from text descriptions or by animating images. Perfect for social media content, product showcases, marketing materials, and creative projects.

## What Can You Create?

- Social media video clips
- Product animations
- Background and ambiance videos
- Marketing and promotional content
- Animated scenes
- Creative visual content

## Creating Your First Video

### Step 1: Open Video Generation

1. Select your company from the sidebar
2. Click **Video** (under the Content Engine menu)
3. Click **Generate Video** (or the **+** button)

### Step 2: Choose Your Approach

You have two main options:

**Text-to-Video:** Describe what you want in words
**Image-to-Video:** Animate a starting image

### Step 3: For Text-to-Video

Write a detailed description of what you want to see:

```
A serene lake at sunset with gentle ripples on the water.
Mountains in the background with soft golden light.
A small boat floating peacefully. Cinematic quality.
```

### Step 4: For Image-to-Video

1. Click **Add Image**
2. Upload a starting image or select from your library
3. Describe how you want it to animate:
```
The clouds slowly moving across the sky,
leaves gently swaying in the wind.
```

### Step 5: Configure Settings

Choose your video options:
- **Duration:** Varies by model (typically 4–30 seconds)
- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 (landscape), 9:16 (portrait/stories), 1:1 (square)
- **Resolution:** 720p or 1080p
- **Model:** Different AI models for different styles

### Step 6: Generate

1. Click **Generate**
2. Wait for processing (can take 1-5 minutes)
3. Preview your video when ready

## Understanding Video Settings

### Duration

Available durations depend on the model you pick. Common choices:

| Duration | Best For |
|----------|----------|
| **5 seconds** | Social clips, GIFs, quick cuts |
| **10 seconds** | Short stories, product showcases |
| **15-30 seconds** | Full scenes, advertisements |

**Note:** Longer videos take more time to generate and use more credits.

### Aspect Ratio

| Ratio | Dimensions | Best For |
|-------|------------|----------|
| **16:9** | Landscape | YouTube, websites, presentations |
| **9:16** | Portrait | TikTok, Instagram Stories, Reels |
| **1:1** | Square | Instagram feed, Facebook |

### Resolution

| Resolution | Quality | File Size |
|------------|---------|-----------|
| **720p** | Good | Smaller |
| **1080p** | High | Larger |

### Motion Settings

Some models offer additional controls:

| Setting | What It Does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Motion Intensity** | Low = subtle, High = dynamic |
| **Camera Motion** | Static, pan, zoom, orbit |
| **Style** | Realistic, cinematic, animated |

## Writing Great Video Prompts

### Be Specific About Movement

The AI needs to know what should move and how:

**Avoid — Vague:**
"A city scene"

**Better — Specific:**
"Cars driving slowly down a city street at night,
neon signs glowing, light rain falling,
camera slowly panning right"

### Include These Elements

| Element | Example |
|---------|---------|
| **Subject** | "A woman in a red dress" |
| **Action** | "walking along the beach" |
| **Environment** | "at sunset, waves gently rolling" |
| **Camera** | "camera following from behind" |
| **Style** | "cinematic film quality" |
| **Mood** | "peaceful and romantic" |

### Example Prompts

**Product showcase:**
```
A sleek smartphone rotating slowly on a white surface.
Soft studio lighting with subtle reflections.
Professional product video style, smooth motion.
```

**Nature scene:**
```
Aerial drone shot flying over a dense green forest.
Morning mist between the trees, golden sunlight
breaking through. Slow, smooth forward movement.
Cinematic quality.
```

**Abstract:**
```
Colorful abstract liquid shapes slowly morphing
and flowing. Deep blues and purples blending.
Smooth, mesmerizing motion. Perfect loop.
```

## Using Images to Start

### Why Start from an Image?

- More control over the visual style
- Use your own product photos
- Create consistent branded content
- Animate existing artwork or designs

### How to Use Image-to-Video

1. Upload or select your starting image
2. Describe what motion you want:
   ```
   The person's hair gently blowing in the wind,
   clouds moving slowly in the background
   ```
3. Configure settings
4. Generate

### First Frame / Last Frame

Some models let you specify both the start and end:

1. Upload a **first frame** (starting image)
2. Upload a **last frame** (ending image)
3. The AI creates a smooth transition between them

## Video Processing Time

Video generation takes longer than images or audio:

| Duration | Typical Wait |
|----------|--------------|
| 5 seconds | 1-2 minutes |
| 10 seconds | 2-3 minutes |
| 15+ seconds | 3-5+ minutes |

You can leave the page while waiting - check back later for your result.

### Checking Status

Look for status indicators:
- **Generating:** Still processing
- **Complete:** Ready to view
- **Failed:** Something went wrong (try again or adjust prompt)

## Tips for Better Videos

### 1. Start Short

Generate 5-second clips first to test your prompt. Once you like the result, try longer versions.

### 2. Keep Movement Simple

AI handles simple, single-direction movement best:
- Slow pan across a scene
- Gradual zoom in
- Single subject moving

Complex multi-element movement often has issues.

### 3. Be Patient

Video generation is computationally intensive. Give it time, and don't generate the same thing multiple times simultaneously.

### 4. Iterate on Your Prompt

If results aren't right:
- Add more specific details
- Simplify what you're asking for
- Try different style keywords
- Use a different model

### 5. Consider Combining Clips

For longer videos:
1. Generate multiple short clips
2. Download them
3. Combine in a video editor

## Common Issues

### "My video looks different than expected"

- Add more descriptive detail
- Specify the style explicitly
- Try a different model
- Reduce the number of moving elements

### "Motion is too fast/slow"

- Adjust the motion intensity setting
- Add "slow" or "gentle" to your prompt
- Shorter videos often have smoother motion

### "Video generation failed"

- Try a simpler prompt
- Reduce video length
- Check that your prompt doesn't violate content policies
- Try again later (might be high demand)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I add audio to generated videos?**
A: Generate video and [audio](/engage/content-engine/audio) separately, then combine in a video editor.

**Q: Why is video generation slow?**
A: Video requires generating many frames and is computationally intensive. It's normal for it to take a few minutes.

**Q: Can I use these videos commercially?**
A: Yes, check your subscription terms for specific commercial use rights.

**Q: What's the maximum video length?**
A: Typically 30 seconds per generation. For longer videos, generate multiple clips and combine them.

**Q: Can I generate videos with specific people?**
A: Generating realistic videos of specific real people isn't supported for privacy and ethical reasons.
