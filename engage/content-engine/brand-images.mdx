Brand Images let you create reusable image collections with consistent styling for your AI-generated content. Define your brand's visual identity once, then use it across all your campaigns.

## What Are Brand Images?

Brand Images are collections that include:

- **Reference Images:** Logos, product photos, icons, and other brand assets
- **Style Prompts:** AI-generated descriptions that maintain visual consistency
- **AI Models:** Preferred image generation models for your brand
- **Frame Templates:** Transparent overlays to brand your generated images

## Creating a Brand Image Collection

### Step 1: Open Brand Images

1. Select your company from the sidebar
2. Click **Brand Images** (under Content Tools)
3. Click **New Brand Image**

### Step 2: Basic Information

- **Name:** A descriptive name (e.g., "Company Marketing", "Product Line A")
- **Description:** Notes about when to use this collection

### Step 3: Add Reference Images

Upload images that represent your brand:

1. Click **Upload Image** in the Images section
2. Select an image from your computer
3. For each image, set:
   - **Type:** Logo, Product, Hero, Icon, Banner, Social Media, Character, or Other
   - **Description:** How this image should be used

**Tip:** Add your logo and key product images so the AI can reference them.

### Step 4: Configure AI Models

Select which AI image generation models to use with this brand:

1. Check the models you want to enable
2. Different models produce different styles
3. You can select multiple models

### Step 5: Generate a Style Prompt

Create a consistent style description for all generated images:

1. Enter a description of your desired style in the text field
2. Click **Generate Style Prompt** (magic wand icon)
3. The AI creates a detailed Midjourney-style prompt
4. Edit as needed

**Example style prompt:**
```
professional aesthetic of a modern tech platform, 
soft pastel color palette with warm tones, 
gentle and playful textures, diffused natural lighting, 
balanced composition with centered focus, 
warm inviting mood --ar 16:9 --style raw --v 6
```

## Creating Frame Templates with AI

Frame templates are transparent PNG overlays that brand your AI-generated images. Use the AI Frame Builder to create them.

### Opening the Frame Builder

1. In the Brand Image dialog, scroll to **Templates**
2. Click **Create with AI**

### Building Your Frame

1. **Select Aspect Ratio:** Choose from presets like Square (1:1), Instagram Story (9:16), Landscape (16:9), etc.
2. **Name Your Frame:** Enter a descriptive name
3. **Describe Your Design:** Chat with the AI to build your frame

**Example prompts:**
- "Create a frame with our company name in the bottom-right corner with a subtle gradient"
- "Add a 'NEW' badge in the top-left corner"
- "Make a professional watermark with slight transparency"

### Frame Design Tips

- **Keep the center empty:** The main image shows through transparent areas
- **Use edges and corners:** Place logos, text, and decorations around the borders
- **Consider contrast:** Use shadows and outlines so elements are readable over any image
- **Test different backgrounds:** The preview shows a checkered pattern for transparency

### Exporting Your Frame

1. Click **Export & Add Template**
2. The frame is saved as a transparent PNG
3. It's automatically added to your templates list

## Using Brand Images in Campaigns

When generating images for content:

1. Select your Brand Image collection
2. The AI uses your:
   - Style prompt for consistent visuals
   - Reference images for context
   - Preferred AI models
3. Apply frame templates to finished images

## Managing Brand Images

### Editing a Collection

1. Click the **three-dot menu** (⋮) on any row
2. Select **Edit**
3. Make your changes
4. Click **Save**

### Deleting a Collection

1. Click the **three-dot menu** (⋮)
2. Select **Delete**
3. Confirm deletion

**Note:** Deleting a brand image collection doesn't delete the actual images, only the collection configuration.

## Best Practices

### 1. Create Purpose-Specific Collections

Instead of one generic collection, create specific ones:
- "Social Media Marketing"
- "Product Announcements"
- "Blog Post Headers"

### 2. Keep Style Prompts Updated

As your brand evolves, update your style prompts to match.

### 3. Use Descriptive Image Labels

When adding reference images, write clear descriptions so the AI knows how to use them:
- **Better:** "Primary logo for dark backgrounds, use in bottom-right corner"
- **Avoid:** "logo2.png"

### 4. Test Frame Templates

Before using frames in production:
1. Generate a test image
2. Apply your frame template
3. Verify it looks good on various image types

## Troubleshooting

### Frame has padding when overlaid

If your frame appears smaller than the image:
- Ensure the frame dimensions match your target aspect ratio
- Regenerate the frame with the AI Frame Builder

### Style prompt not working

If generated images don't match your style:
- Make the style prompt more specific
- Include concrete details about colors, lighting, and mood
- Regenerate the style prompt with more context

### Reference images not appearing

- Check that images uploaded successfully
- Ensure images have descriptions
- Try re-uploading the image
