Send a single, tracked email to everyone on a Smart List — a newsletter, an announcement, or a one-time promotion — with built-in open and click tracking.

## What Is a Marketing Campaign?

A marketing campaign is a **one-time broadcast email** sent from a sender profile to the contacts on one Smart List. It's the right tool when you want to send *one* message to *many* people at once.

**How it differs from other features:**

- **Marketing Campaign** — one email, sent to a whole list, once. Best for newsletters and announcements.
- **[Sequence](/engage/sequences)** — a multi-step, per-person workflow (email, LinkedIn, SMS, and more) with delays between steps. Best for outreach that follows up over time.
- **[Content Engine](/engage/content-engine)** — creates the content itself; it doesn't send bulk email.

## Creating a Campaign

1. Select your company from the sidebar
2. Click **Marketing Campaigns** (under Engage)
3. On the **Campaigns** tab, click **New Campaign**
4. Fill in the details:
   - **Campaign Name** — internal label; recipients never see it
   - **Subject** — the email subject line
   - **Sender Profile** — who the email comes from (required)
   - **From Name** and **Reply-To** — optional overrides
   - **Smart List** — the audience for this campaign
   - **Advanced → Email field** — pick which list column holds the email address (auto-detected in most cases)
5. Build the email body:
   - **Visual Builder** — a drag-and-drop email editor
   - or the **HTML editor** for a WYSIWYG experience
6. Choose when to send:
   - **Send Now** — begins sending immediately
   - **Schedule for Later** — pick a date, time, and timezone
   - *(Optional)* set per-day **send windows** so email only goes out during chosen hours
7. *(Recommended)* Send yourself a **test** first to check formatting and links
8. Click **Save**, **Save & Schedule**, or **Save & Send Now**

> **Sender tip:** For bulk sending, use a dedicated sending profile (such as one backed by a high-volume email service). Personal inbox profiles work but show a warning — they aren't built for large sends.

## Using Templates

Reuse a proven layout instead of starting from scratch:

1. Open **Templates** and click **New Template**
2. Build the template like a campaign, putting `{{content}}` where the per-campaign message goes
3. To use it, choose **Use template** from that template's menu — the campaign form opens with its styling and defaults applied

See [Templates](/engage/marketing-campaigns/campaign-templates) for the full details.

## Campaign Statuses

| Status | What It Means |
|--------|---------------|
| **Draft** | Created but not sending — edit freely |
| **Scheduled** | Queued for a future send |
| **Sending** | Actively going out (paced automatically) |
| **Sent** | Completed — recipients and metrics are locked |
| **Failed** | A send error occurred — you can fix and retry |
| **Cancelled** | A scheduled send was cancelled — edit and reschedule |

Sends are paced automatically so your campaign goes out at a steady rate rather than all at once. Recipients are captured from the list when sending begins, so later changes to the list don't affect a campaign already in flight.

## Tracking Performance

Open a campaign to see its results, and use the metrics bar for an at-a-glance view across all campaigns:

| Metric | What It Tells You |
|--------|-------------------|
| **Sent** | Emails successfully delivered |
| **Opened** | Recipients who opened, with open rate |
| **Clicked** | Recipients who clicked a link, with click rate |
| **Failed** | Delivery errors |
| **Skipped** | Rows with no email, duplicates, or suppressed contacts |

The campaign detail view also includes a per-recipient table (search by email) and a timeline of sends, opens, and clicks.
