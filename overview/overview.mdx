These are the items at the top of the sidebar, above the feature sections. Everything else in the platform is built on them — an AI employee with a knowledge base is what powers agents, sequences, content, and automations.

## In This Section

- **[AI Employees](/overview/employees)** — Create and customize the AI team members that power chat, agents, and automations.
- **[Chat](/overview/chat)** — Talk directly with your AI employees to get work done, ask questions, and think out loud.
- **[Scheduled](/overview/scheduled)** — Give an employee standing work that runs once, on a schedule, or continuously.
- **[Knowledge Base](/overview/knowledge-base)** — Upload the documents, websites, and files that train your AI employees.
- **[Dashboard](/overview/dashboard)** — Your analytics home: activity, usage, and performance across every feature you use.

## How These Fit Together

An **AI employee** is a persona with its own instructions, model, and access to your data. You give it knowledge through the **Knowledge Base**, work with it in **Chat**, and hand it standing work in **Scheduled**. The **Dashboard** shows what all of it added up to.

The same employees show up throughout the rest of the platform — as the brain behind an [agent](/inbound/agents), the writer behind [content](/engage/content-engine), and the researcher behind [Smart List](/find-enrich/smart-lists) actions.
