The rest of the platform reaches out. This section is what's waiting when someone arrives — an agent that answers at 2am, a landing page behind an ad, a full website, or a custom app.

## In This Section

- **[Agents](/inbound/agents)** — Chatbots on your website, by phone, and in messaging apps that answer questions and capture leads.
- **[WhatsApp](/inbound/agents/whatsapp)** — Connect an agent to WhatsApp so customers can message your business directly.
- **[Websites](/inbound/websites)** — Build complete marketing websites with a built-in content management system.
- **[Landing Pages](/inbound/landing-pages)** — Single pages for campaigns and lead capture, built with an AI chat builder.
- **[App Builder](/inbound/app-builder)** — Create custom web apps with AI, connect them to your data, and publish them.

## Websites, Landing Pages, or Apps?

All three are built by describing what you want to an AI builder, and all three publish to a custom domain. They differ in what they're for:

| | Best for | Shape |
|---|----------|-------|
| **[Websites](/inbound/websites)** | Your main marketing site | Many pages, navigation, a built-in CMS |
| **[Landing Pages](/inbound/landing-pages)** | One campaign, one offer | A single focused page |
| **[App Builder](/inbound/app-builder)** | Something interactive | An app with data behind it |

If it needs a menu and more than a few pages, build a website. If it's the destination for one ad or one email, build a landing page. If people need to *do* something rather than read something, build an app.
