The Content Engine helps you create and manage marketing content using AI. Plan your content strategy, maintain brand consistency, and generate blog posts, social media content, and more.

---

## What Can You Create?

- Blog posts and articles
- Social media posts
- Email newsletters
- Landing page copy
- Ad copy
- Video scripts
- And more!

---

## Understanding the Content Workflow

The Content Engine guides you through a strategic content creation process:

1. **Ideal Customer Profile** → Who are you creating content for?
2. **Content Strategy** → What topics will you cover?
3. **Brand Voice** → How should your content sound?
4. **Brand Images** → What visuals represent your brand?
5. **Campaigns** → How will you organize your content?
6. **Content** → The actual pieces you create

You can start anywhere, but working through these steps makes your content more effective.

---

## Step 1: Define Your Ideal Customer

Before creating content, know who you're creating it for.

### Creating a Customer Profile

1. Go to **Content Engine**
2. Click the **Ideal Customer Profile** tab
3. Click **Create Profile**
4. Fill in details about your target customer:
   - Demographics (age, location, job title)
   - Pain points (what problems do they have?)
   - Goals (what do they want to achieve?)
   - How they make decisions
   - Where they consume content
5. Save your profile

### Why This Matters

When AI generates content, it uses your customer profile to:
- Choose the right tone and language
- Address relevant pain points
- Focus on benefits that matter to them

---

## Step 2: Plan Your Content Strategy

A content strategy defines what you'll create and why.

### Creating a Strategy

1. Click the **Content Strategy** tab
2. Click **Create Strategy**
3. Define content pillars for each stage of your funnel:

**TOFU (Top of Funnel) - Awareness:**
- Educational content
- Industry trends
- General problem-solving

**MOFU (Middle of Funnel) - Consideration:**
- How-to guides
- Comparison content
- Case studies

**BOFU (Bottom of Funnel) - Decision:**
- Product demos
- Customer testimonials
- Pricing and ROI content

4. Save your strategy

### Using Your Strategy

When creating content, you can select which funnel stage it's for. The AI will adjust the messaging accordingly.

---

## Step 3: Establish Your Brand Voice

Your brand voice is how your content sounds - the personality, tone, and style.

### Creating a Brand Voice

1. Click the **Brand Voice** tab
2. Click **Create Brand Voice**
3. Define:
   - **Personality:** Professional? Casual? Playful? Authoritative?
   - **Tone:** Friendly, formal, conversational, etc.
   - **Writing style:** Simple, technical, storytelling, etc.
   - **Words to use:** Terminology and phrases you like
   - **Words to avoid:** Language that doesn't fit your brand
4. Save

### Example Brand Voice

```
Personality: Friendly expert
Tone: Warm but professional
Style: Clear and direct, avoid jargon

DO use:
- "You" and "your" to speak directly to readers
- Simple explanations for complex topics
- Real examples and stories

DON'T use:
- Corporate buzzwords like "synergy" or "leverage"
- Overly casual language like "gonna" or "wanna"
- Fear-based marketing tactics
```

## Step 4: Upload Brand Images

Keep your visual assets organized and ready for AI-generated content.

### Adding Brand Images

1. Click the **Brand Images** tab
2. Click **Upload Images**
3. Add your:
   - Logos (different versions)
   - Product photos
   - Team photos
   - Marketing graphics
   - Stock images you frequently use
4. Tag images for easy finding later

### Using Brand Images

When generating content with images, you can reference these assets or let AI incorporate them.

---

## Step 5: Organize with Campaigns

Campaigns group related content pieces together.

### Creating a Campaign

1. Click the **Campaigns** tab
2. Click **Create Campaign**
3. Fill in:
   - **Name:** "Spring Product Launch" or "Q1 Thought Leadership"
   - **Description:** What this campaign is about
   - **Date range:** When content will be published
4. Save

### Why Use Campaigns?

- Keep related content together
- Track performance by campaign
- Plan content calendars
- Coordinate team efforts

---

## Step 6: Create Your Content

Now you're ready to create actual content pieces!

### Creating New Content

1. Click the **Content** tab
2. Click **Create Content**
3. Choose:
   - **Type:** Blog post, social post, email, etc.
   - **Campaign:** Which campaign it belongs to
   - **Brand Voice:** Which voice to use
   - **Target audience:** Which customer profile
4. Either:
   - **Write it yourself** - Use the editor
   - **Generate with AI** - Describe what you want

### AI Content Generation

To have AI write for you:

1. Describe what you want:
   ```
   Write a blog post about the benefits of automated email marketing for small businesses. Include 3 main points and a call to action.
   ```
2. Click **Generate**
3. Review the draft
4. Edit as needed
5. Save

### Editing Content

Use the content editor to:
- Format text (headings, bold, lists)
- Add images
- Include links
- Preview how it looks

### Content Status

Track where each piece is in your workflow:

| Status | What It Means |
|--------|---------------|
| **Draft** | Work in progress |
| **Review** | Ready for team review |
| **Approved** | Ready to publish |
| **Published** | Live and available |
| **Archived** | No longer active |

## Tips for Better AI-Generated Content

### 1. Be Specific in Your Prompts

❌ "Write about marketing"
✅ "Write a 500-word blog post about 5 email marketing mistakes small businesses make, with tips to fix each one"

### 2. Provide Context

Tell the AI:
- Who the audience is
- What action you want readers to take
- What tone to use
- Any specific points to include or avoid

### 3. Always Edit AI Content

AI provides a great starting point, but always:
- Check facts and statistics
- Add your unique insights
- Ensure it matches your brand voice
- Remove anything that feels generic

### 4. Use Templates

If you create similar content regularly, save successful pieces as templates.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I generate content without setting up profiles and strategies?**
A: Yes! But your content will be more effective if you complete the earlier steps.

**Q: How long does AI content generation take?**
A: Usually just a few seconds for short pieces, up to a minute for longer articles.

**Q: Can the AI write in different languages?**
A: Yes, specify the language in your prompt or brand voice settings.

**Q: Is AI-generated content unique?**
A: Yes, each generation is unique. However, always review before publishing.

**Q: Can I schedule content to publish later?**
A: The platform helps you create and organize content. For publishing, you'll typically export or copy to your publishing platform.
